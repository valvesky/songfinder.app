# Privacy policy: findSong.app

findSong.app does not collect, store or sell personal data, has no accounts, and runs no servers.

When you press "Identify song" the extension records about 10 seconds of audio from the video
you picked and converts it on your device into a spectral fingerprint (a list of frequency
peaks, not audio). That fingerprint is sent over HTTPS to a music recognition service
(`amp.shazam.com`) to look up the track, and the title/artist are shown to you.

Nothing is sent unless you press the button. The audio itself, the page URL and your browsing
history never leave your browser. The extension keeps nothing after the result is shown.
