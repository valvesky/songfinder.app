Made for pages full of videos

Small, quick and out of the way until you need it.

◉

A button on every video

A small glass badge in the top-right corner. One click names the song. Switch it off from the popup if you prefer.

▢

Knows which video

The video it will listen to gets outlined, so it is never a guess. Hover a card in the popup to see which is which.

▶

Starts it for you

Paused or muted? It plays, unmutes, listens, then puts things back the way they were.

↔

Pick the part to hear

Drag two dots on the timeline, or type a start and end time, for the moment the song actually plays.

∅

Skips GIFs

Videos with no audio track, like Tenor and Discord GIFs served as MP4, are ignored. No clutter.

⌁

Nothing to run

Everything happens inside the extension. No server, no account. Only a tiny audio fingerprint is sent for lookup.

How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds.

01

Spot the video

It finds every video with sound on the page, including ones inside iframes, and badges them.

02

Listen

It records the audio of just that video, 10 seconds by default, and turns it into a fingerprint on your device.

03

Name it

The fingerprint is matched and the title and artist appear on the video, with a link to the track.

Install

Not on the stores yet. Load it unpacked in a minute.

Brave · Chrome · Edge

  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Open brave://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension/ folder.

Firefox

  1. Build the Firefox package:
git clone https://github.com/valvesky/songfinder.app
cd songfinder.app && tools/build.sh
  1. Open about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on.
  2. Pick dist/firefox/manifest.json.

Recognition uses Shazam's unofficial endpoint, so it may change over time. songfinder.app is not affiliated with Shazam.

Private by design

The video, the page and your history never leave your browser. Only a spectral fingerprint of the chosen segment is sent, and only when you press the button.
