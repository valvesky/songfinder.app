Privacy policy

songfinder.app does not collect, store or sell personal data, has no accounts, and runs no servers.

When you press “Identify song” the extension records the chosen segment of audio (10 seconds by default) from the video you picked and converts it, on your device, into a spectral fingerprint: a list of frequency peaks, not audio. That fingerprint is sent over HTTPS to a music recognition service (amp.shazam.com) to look up the track, and the title and artist are shown to you.

Nothing is sent unless you press the button. The audio itself, the page URL and your browsing history never leave your browser. The only thing the extension stores is your overlay on/off setting, in your browser’s own extension storage.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
